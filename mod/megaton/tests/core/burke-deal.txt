# Burke's pitch, taken at face value: hear it, refuse, come back, accept, fit the

#   python3 mod/megaton/tests/core/run_all.py burke-deal      (needs PipBoyAvailableAtGameStart=1)
# Expected: Q_BURKE 0 -> 1 (heard) -> 2 (accepted, charge in the pack, MG_F_HAS_CHARGE)
# -> 3 (fitted); BOMB 2; BURKE 4 and the man standing on BURKE_RENDEZVOUS; karma -10.
