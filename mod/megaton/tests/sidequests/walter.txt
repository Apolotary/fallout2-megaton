# Walter's leaks, the ordinary way: take the job, get his piece of junk, close the
# three leaks by all three routes, get paid, then sell and give away scrap.
#   python3 mod/megaton/tests/sidequests/run_all.py walter
# Test character: Repair 5, $150. Rig commands (GVAR 690/691/692) are explained in stage.py.
# Item PIDs: 98 Junk, 75 Tool. Skill 13 = Repair. 16777379 = Walter's proto (0x010000A3).
#
# Expected: Q_WATER 0 -> 1 (accepted) -> 2 (third leak) -> 3 (paid);
#           LEAK_BITS 1 -> 3 -> 7; money 150 -> 350 -> 375 -> 425; karma 5 -> 7.

dude @WALTER/3
wait 500
talk @WALTER
wait 2500
expect state dialog=1
shot 01-greeting
# 1 = "You look like you could use a hand."
key 1
wait 1500
shot 02-offer
# 1 = "Two hundred. I'll see to it."
key 1
wait 1500
shot 03-accepted
expect gvar $GVAR_MG_Q_WATER $MG_Q_WATER_ACCEPTED
# 1 = "How do I patch a pipe?"  (he hands over one Junk)
key 1
wait 1500
shot 04-how-to
# 1 = "Where are the leaks, again?"
key 1
wait 1500
shot 05-where
# 2 = "On my way."
key 2
wait 1500
expect state dialog=0
setgvar 691 98
setgvar 690 9
wait 400
expect gvar 692 1

# --- leak 1: Repair 5 is hopeless (the Repair skill used on the pipe; a plain click only
# looks at it, because the pipe art has no "use" action)
dude @LEAK1/3
wait 300
skill 13 @LEAK1
wait 2500
shot 06-hopeless
expect gvar $GVAR_MG_LEAK_BITS 0
# Repair 15: Walter's piece of junk used ON the pipe from the hand slot, through the real
# inventory (the junk is the newest item, top of the list): closed, the junk is used up, 15 XP
setgvar 691 13
setgvar 692 10
setgvar 690 2
wait 400
key i
wait 1500
move 160 58
wait 200
press 1
wait 400
move 275 325
wait 400
press 0
wait 800
shot 07a-junk-in-hand
key esc
wait 1000
click 360 440
wait 600
clicktile @LEAK1 0 -30
wait 5000
shot 07-closed-with-junk
expect gvar $GVAR_MG_LEAK_BITS $MG_LEAK_1
expect noobj @LEAK1 0 pid=0x0200073B
expect obj @LEAK1 0 pid=0x0200019F
setgvar 691 98
setgvar 690 9
wait 400
expect gvar 692 0
setgvar 690 11
wait 400
expect gvar 692 15

# --- leak 2: Repair 15, no junk: told what is missing

# --- leak 3: Repair 25 + tool = 35: closed bare-handed, 25 XP, the job is done
