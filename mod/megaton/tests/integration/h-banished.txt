# (h) "I MEAN IT. THAT BOMB IS GOING OFF.", said to the sheriff: the town turns hostile (design
# brief 5.4), but not by a rifle burst in the player's face. Simms marches him out of the gate
# first; the town is hostile from that moment, shouts its warning, and for eight seconds nobody
# shoots (megaton.h, mg_in_grace). The road is two hexes behind the player.
# Before: the first burst killed the 44-hit-point test character as the conversation closed.
# Expected: no hostility while the talk is open; after it TOWN_HOSTILE 1, the player alive on
# ENTRY, no fight for the first seconds; he steps back onto the exit grid and is on the world
# map (map name empty). Coming straight back in, the alarm starts again: no fight for the first
# seconds either, then the gate's defenders attack (combat 1).

setgvar $GVAR_MG_FLAGS 262145
wait 2000
dude @SIMMS/3
wait 800
talk @SIMMS
wait 3000
key 4
wait 1500
# "I mean it. That bomb is going off."
key 2
wait 1500
shot 01-raider-with-manners
expect state dialog=1 combat=0
expect gvar $GVAR_MG_TOWN_HOSTILE 0
key 1
wait 3500
shot 02-outside-and-shouted-at
expect state dialog=0 combat=0 dudeTile=@ENTRY
expect gvar $GVAR_MG_TOWN_HOSTILE $MG_TOWN_IS_HOSTILE
expect gvar $GVAR_TOWN_REP_MEGATON -10
wait 2000
expect state combat=0
# --- the way out is behind him
clicktile @GATE_EXIT
wait 8000
shot 03-world-map
expect state map=
# --- and straight back in: warned again, then shot at
click 237 181
wait 8000
expect state mapIndex=151 combat=0
shot 04-back-in-shouted-at-again
wait 12000
shot 05-they-meant-it
expect state combat=1
expect msg That's the last warning this town gives.
