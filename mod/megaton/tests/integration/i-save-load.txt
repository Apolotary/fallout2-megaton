# (i) PERSISTENCE, save and load in the middle of three quests on the real town: Simms'
# offer taken, Moira's notes bought, Walter's job taken and one leak closed, Gob befriended.
# Save, wreck the state by hand, load: everything is as it was saved, and the quests go on
# from there (the bomb is disarmed and reported after the load: local variables, timers and
# the scripts' exported pointers all survived).
# Keys: 318 = F4 (save screen), 319 = F5 (load screen); Enter picks the slot, a second
# Enter accepts the description.
# Expected after the load: Q_SIMMS 1, Q_WATER 1, LEAK_BITS 1, flags WELD_GREETED |
# GOB_FRIEND | MOIRA_NOTES | SIMMS_MET (262155), the gate open, leak 1 without steam and
# leak 2 with it, the notes in the pack, money 100; then Q_SIMMS 3 and the house.
