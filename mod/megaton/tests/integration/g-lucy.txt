# (g) LUCY WEST'S LETTER on the real town: Lucy by the saloon door hands over the letter,
# the Pip-Boy lists it, the letter is examined in the inventory, the caravan trader

# sealed, and Lucy answers "What does the letter say?" herself.)
# Expected: Q_LETTER 0 -> 1 -> 2 -> 3; flag HAS_LETTER while carrying it; money 150 - 30;
# XP 100; karma +5; two Nuka-Colas; no letter left in the pack.
