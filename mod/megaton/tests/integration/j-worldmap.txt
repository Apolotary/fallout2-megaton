# (j) A NORMAL NEW GAME, on the archive as shipped (group int-ship, --map arvillag.map: the
# game begins in Arroyo village as it does after the Temple of Trials, which is too long
# for a test). The player leaves by the bridge, finds an unnamed circle south-east of the
# village on the world map, walks to it (about a day) and steps into Megaton on foot: no
# starting kit, no second movie, the gate shut and Weld waiting as for anybody. Then the
# round trip the other groups never made: out of Megaton, back to Arroyo village, and to
# Megaton again, which is as he left it.
# DICE: three legs on the world map; a random encounter on the way stops the scenario.
# test.py dismisses Arroyo's own Vault-suit movie (--skip-movies 1, run_all.py).
# Tiles: 30896 is an exit-grid hex of arvillag.map (to the bridge), 26734 one of
# arbridge.map (to the world map). World-map clicks are window pixels; "key home" centres
# the map on the party first, so they are the same on every run.
# Expected: KIT_GIVEN 0 in Arroyo, 2 (not due) in Megaton; map 151 entered at ENTRY; flag
# WELD_GREETED and the gate open after the greeting, and still so after the round trip;
# RESTOCK_DAY moved on (the trip takes more than three days in all).
