# (c) AFTER THE BLAST: the detonation ends on the world map; the player then clicks the party
# marker (or walks anywhere and into wilderness). Found by the playtest: standing on one of the
# hexes beside Burke at the fire when he said "Do it", the first wilderness map threw the player
# straight back to the world map: the engine kept his last tile, and on the desert encounter
# maps that tile is an exit grid. mgburke.ssl now moves him to the entry hex before the map
# is left.
# Expected: world map after the blast (state map= empty); after the click the desert map is
# entered and STAYS entered: the clock moves on (it stood still on the bounced world map).
# The job is set up by hand: the charge fitted, Burke at the fire.

setgvar $GVAR_MG_FLAGS $MG_F_WELD_GREETED
wait 1500
setgvar $GVAR_MG_Q_BURKE $MG_Q_BURKE_PLANTED
setgvar $GVAR_MG_BOMB $MG_BOMB_RIGGED
setgvar $GVAR_MG_BURKE $MG_BURKE_RENDEZVOUS
wait 3000
# the hex that bounced: one step east of him, two out
dude @BURKE_RENDEZVOUS/1/2
wait 2500
talk @BURKE_RENDEZVOUS
wait 3000
key 1
wait 1500
key 1
wait 1500
key 1
wait 12000
shot 01-world-map
expect gvar $GVAR_MG_BOMB $MG_BOMB_DETONATED
click 237 181
wait 6000
shot 02-in-the-desert
expect state map=DESERT1.MAP time>338520
wait 6000
shot 03-still-in-the-desert
expect state map=DESERT1.MAP time>338580
