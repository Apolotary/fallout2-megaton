# (h) THE TOWN FORGIVES, and so does the man who was hit. Day 8 on the clock (rig); the
# player punches a settler of the congregation at the pool (rig: Unarmed +200 so the
# punch lands), the town turns hostile, and he is off the map before anybody's turn comes
# (a step onto the exit grid). On the world map the stamp is put back to day 0, as if the
# blow had been struck a week ago; then he walks in again.
# Expected after re-entry: TOWN_HOSTILE 0, HOSTILE_DAY 0, AMNESTY 1, reputation -15, the
# "short memory" line and no fight, not even from the settler who was hit: his personal
# grudge lapses with the town's (megaton.h, mg_has_grudge; before that fix he opened fire
# on sight and the town was hostile again the moment the player defended himself).
# Simms, the settler and Moira all talk.
