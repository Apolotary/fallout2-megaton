# (g) WALTER'S LEAKS on the real town: the job, his lump of junk, a botched attempt, one
# leak closed with junk and little skill, two by Repair, payment, and the scrap he buys
# afterwards (paid, then given away for karma).
# Narg's Repair is about 5: the rig raises it to 17 (the junk route needs 15), later to 42
# (35 closes a leak without materials).
# Expected: Q_WATER 0 -> 1 -> 2 -> 3; LEAK_BITS 0 -> 1 -> 3 -> 7; the steam leaves each
# leak; $200, karma +5, reputation +5; scrap: 5 XP a piece either way, $25 a piece sold, +1 karma a piece
# given away; XP 15 + 25 + 25 + 100 + 3 x 5 = 180; money 150 + 200 + 25 = 375; karma 5 + 2 = 7.

dude @IN_PLANT_MACHINE_HALL
wait 1500
shot 01-water-plant
talk @WALTER
wait 4000
expect state dialog=1
shot 02-walter
# "You look like you could use a hand."
key 1
wait 1500
shot 03-three-leaks
# "Two hundred. I'll see to it."
key 1
wait 1500
expect gvar $GVAR_MG_Q_WATER $MG_Q_WATER_ACCEPTED
# "How do I patch a pipe?"
key 1
wait 1500
shot 04-how-to
# "Where are the leaks, again?"
key 1
wait 1500
shot 05-where
key 2
wait 1500
expect state dialog=0
setgvar 691 98
setgvar 692 2
wait 1200
expect gvar 680 1

wait 1500

# --- leak 1, by the empty house: no skill at all
dude @LEAK1/1
wait 1000
shot 07-leak-by-the-house

# while the lower pipe yields mouse clicks inside the player's see-through circle.

wait 400
key 8
wait 500
clicktile @LEAK1 8 -78
wait 5000
shot 08-made-it-worse

wait 400

wait 500

expect gvar $GVAR_MG_LEAK_BITS $MG_LEAK_1

expect obj @LEAK1 0 pid=0x0200019F
setgvar 691 98
setgvar 692 2
wait 1200
expect gvar 680 0
